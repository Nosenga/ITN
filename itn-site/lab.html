IPv4 Addressing & Subnetting Lab

Interactive calculator, step-by-step solutions, and unlimited generated practice — three difficulty levels, instant feedback, full worked answers.

Running in-page self-test…

1 · Calculator

Enter an IPv4 address and either a dotted mask (255.255.255.0) or a CIDR prefix (/24). You can also type them together as 192.168.1.10/24 in the address box and leave the mask empty. Special prefixes (/31, /32) are handled correctly.

2 · Step-by-step solutions

After every calculation you get the full working: prefix → mask, block-size arithmetic, network and broadcast identification, host range and usable count — shown for both dotted-decimal and CIDR input.

The worked solution appears here after each calculation.

3–5 · Practice & quiz

Questions are generated randomly each time (so answers can’t be memorised) from verified templates: subnet masks, network and broadcast addresses, usable ranges, host counts, choosing a subnet size, dividing a network, and VLSM. Pick a level and question types, then answer one question at a time — you get automatic checking, an explanation of a wrong answer, the complete correct solution, running accuracy, and the chance to retry anything you missed. Pick Mixed as the difficulty for a revision set that draws questions at random from all three levels.

Every set is newly generated and answer-checked before it is shown.

The calculator’s arithmetic (including /0, /8, /16, /24, /30, /31 and /32) is covered by automated tests in site-build/test-ipv4.js, and the badge above re-runs the calculator’s self-test in your browser every time this page loads. The question generator only admits questions whose answers it can re-derive independently. Lifetime accuracy counters are stored only in this browser (localStorage key itn-lab-stats-v1) — no accounts, no servers, nothing syncs between devices, and clearing site data resets them; individual questions and answers are not saved. Everything runs locally — no paid services or external APIs, and your answers never leave the page.
